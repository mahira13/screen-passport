# Screen Passport: product vision

**From the screen to the streets.**

Screen Passport is a cinematic discovery, travel and social experience. People don't just find where a film or series was shot: they are inspired to go there, plan cinematic journeys, collect memories and share them.

## Core loop

Discover a location → get curious → start a collection or challenge → plan a journey → visit → collect stamps → share → inspire others.

## Four pillars

1. **Discover:** filming locations near me (5, 10, 25, 50 km or anywhere); explore by film, series, city or country; curated collections.
2. **Explore:** follow collections ("Dark filming locations in Germany", "Berlin through cinema"); a map of real locations; routes and trips across several locations. Create a trip from any collection and invite others to join.
3. **Collect:** a personal, anonymous Screen Passport; check in at real locations to earn stamps; collection progress and badges.
4. **Connect:** challenge friends to complete a collection; share passports, trips and journeys; take your own photo at the real location (scene postcard).

## Structure

- **Home:** "What cinematic adventure do you want to go on?" with three entry points: Find filming locations near me · Explore a film or series · Discover a cinematic collection.
- **Discover**, **Collections**, **Map**, **My Passport** as the main navigation.

## Principles

- Nearby discovery stays a core feature; collections never replace it.
- One physical location can belong to many films and series.
- Collections and challenges are one system: any collection can be played as a challenge.
- Only published, verified locations appear as confirmed filming locations. Unverified or approximate information is clearly labelled and never used for check-ins.
- Every share leads to a real, public page that works without login.
- Anonymous passports, no mandatory accounts, minimal data.
- No film stills, posters scraped from the web, show logos, show fonts or script quotes. "Recreate a scene" means the user's own photo at the real place.
- A polished, cinematic, editorial feel, not a streaming catalogue or SaaS dashboard.
- Never rebuild working features without a reason; extend them.
